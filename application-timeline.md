- [ ] Decide on a tutor / speaking partner (Writing and Speaking are at 5.5)

- [ ] **Windows open:** TU Dresden M.Sc. CS opens 1 Apr; TU Berlin opens ~2 Apr (unofficial) — submit early if documents are ready

## Phase 2: Admission → Visa (Jul–Sep 2027)

### Jul–Aug 2027
- [ ] Receive first admission decision(s)
- [ ] Accept preferred offer (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung issued)
- [ ] Apply to Studentenwerk dorm immediately on receiving Immatrikulationsbescheinigung
- [ ] Begin Sperrkonto setup: transfer €11,208+ to blocked account (Fintiba, Expatrio, Deutsche Bank)
  - Processing: 2–4 weeks
- [ ] Begin health insurance setup: TK, DAK, or AOK student tariff (~€110–120/month as student)

### Sep 2027
- [ ] Book student visa appointment at German Embassy Vietnam (Hanoi or HCMC)
  - Required documents: valid passport, Immatrikulationsbescheinigung (or admission letter), Sperrkonto proof, IELTS, APS, health insurance proof, passport photos, application form, motivation letter, biometrics
  - Wait time: 4–8 weeks for appointment; book early
- [ ] Submit student visa (Visum zu Studienzwecken, §16b AufenthG)

## Phase 3: Relocation (Sep–Oct 2027)

### Sep–Oct 2027
- [ ] Visa issued (typically 4–8 weeks processing)
- [ ] Book flights to Germany
- [ ] Arrange temporary accommodation if dorm not yet available (WG-Gesucht, Homelike)
- [ ] Ship essentials (or buy in Germany; often cheaper to buy locally)

### Oct 2027 — M.Sc. Starts
- [ ] Anmeldung (register address at Bürgeramt within 2 weeks of arrival)
- [ ] Convert Sperrkonto to regular account (or open Girokonto at a German bank)
- [ ] Enrol at university (Einschreiben / Immatrikulation)
- [ ] Register at Krankenkasse as a student
- [ ] Apply for Werkstudent roles (start 2nd semester or late 1st semester after settling in)
