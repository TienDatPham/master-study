### Universität Hamburg — M.Sc. Informatik

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Language | German |
| Duration | 4 semesters (120 LP) |
| Location | Hamburg |
| Tuition | Free (Hamburg state) |
| Semester fee | ~€370 (incl. HVV Semesterticket — Hamburg transport) |
| Deadline | 15 May (non-EU); 15 July (EU/German degree) |
| Start | Winter semester |
| Language proof | German B.Sc. accepted in lieu of DSH/TestDaF |

**Admission requirements:**
- B.Sc. Informatik or equivalent (180 LP / ≥3 years)
- Strong CS background assessed holistically
- German: DSH-2 or TestDaF 4 × 4 — **waived for German B.Sc. holders**

**Dan's fit:** Clean fit. German B.Sc. covers language requirement. Strong CS background (30 LP Math, 22 LP Theo CS, 28 LP practical) meets Hamburg's holistic assessment. No GRE, no aptitude test.

**Hamburg as a city:**
- Rent: €800–1,100/month (1-room flat)
- Studentenwerk Hamburg dorms: €300–500/month; waitlist ~1–2 semesters
- Large Vietnamese community; major international port city
- Career: strong in logistics tech, shipping/maritime IT, media tech, startups

**Verdict:** Good fit, strong city for career. Higher living cost than Dresden but good market.

## German Language Note for Dan

Dan completed his entire B.Sc. in German. His German is at B2+ level. To maximise German-taught program options and strengthen his long-term integration:
- Consider a Goethe-Zertifikat C1 or TestDaF before arrival (not required if German B.Sc. is accepted, but strengthens applications and helps with job market)
- German C1 is generally expected by German employers (Mittelstand, public sector) even if the M.Sc. was partly in English
