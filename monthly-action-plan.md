## Nov 2026 — IELTS Month 1: diagnose and build foundations

- [ ] Writing: 1 Task 2 + 1 Task 1 per week; extract Key Vocab and Diamond Vocab from 2 sample essays each
- [ ] Speaking: record Part 1 and Part 2 answers daily and note where you stall
- [ ] Reading: 1 Cambridge test per week with a keyword table for every wrong answer
- [ ] Listening: 3 Cambridge tests per week, plus 1 Part 3 dictation per week
- [ ] Start 20–30 min/day of enjoyable English input (podcasts, YouTube, TV)
- [ ] Build the shortlist spreadsheet: deadline, portal, documents, fee, status per university

## Dec 2026 — IELTS Month 2: structured skill-building

- [ ] Writing: 2 Task 2 + 1 Task 1 per week; get feedback on each and rewrite
- [ ] Speaking: 3 mock Part 2+3 sessions per week with a partner or tutor
- [ ] Reading: 2 tests per week; drill your weakest question type
- [ ] Draft the CV (academic + professional, with PMP, UiPath ADPv1, Claude CCA-F)
- [ ] Request 2 recommendation letters now; give recommenders 6–8 weeks
- [ ] Order certified copies of the TU Dresden Zeugnis and transcript

## Jan 2027 — IELTS Month 3: intensive gap-closing

- [ ] **Book the March IELTS Academic seat** (Hanoi or HCMC); seats fill early
- [ ] Writing: 3 essays per week with feedback; focus on Lexical Resource and Grammatical Range
- [ ] Speaking: record 3–4 times per week; work on pronunciation and intonation
- [ ] Reading and Listening: timed full tests only
- [ ] Take a full 4-skill mock at the end of the month. If Writing and Speaking are still below 6.0, plan on TU Berlin (IELTS 6.5) as your safe target
- [ ] Draft motivation letters (one base letter, then one tailored version per university)

## Feb 2027 — IELTS Month 4: mocks and consolidation

- [ ] One full 4-skill mock exam per week under real conditions; analyse every error
- [ ] Build a personal bank of sentence structures and transitions that work for you
- [ ] Drill Part 2 cue cards across all 5 topic groups
- [ ] Re-verify every university deadline and requirement for WiSe 2027/28 on the official sites
- [ ] Decide whether to apply for APS based on the written answers from universities; it takes 6–10 weeks, so start now if any university requires it
- [ ] Finish motivation letters and CV; collect the recommendation letters

## Mar 2027 — IELTS Month 5: exam month

- [ ] 2 full mocks per week early in the month; 1 essay per week; daily Speaking warm-ups
- [ ] Final week: no new material; review your vocab bank and best essays; prioritise sleep
- [ ] **Sit IELTS Academic**
- [ ] When the result arrives (about 3–13 days later), decide: retake in April or proceed
- [ ] Prepare the application portals: create accounts at uni-assist or the direct portals, upload standard documents

## Apr 2027 — buffer and applications

- [ ] If any score is short of the target (7.0, or 6.5 for TU Berlin): book and sit the **IELTS retake**, results before the May deadlines
- [ ] Optional: sit the GRE General Test (about USD 220), recommended for TU Dresden; score reports take about 10 days
- [ ] **1 Apr:** TU Dresden window opens; TU Berlin opens ~2 Apr. Submit early so there is time to fix document problems
- [ ] Hold the application budget: IELTS retake about €190, GRE about €200, APS about €80

## Aug 2027 — admission to visa preparation

- [ ] Withdraw or decline the remaining applications politely

## Sep 2027 — visa

- [ ] Book the German Embassy appointment (Hanoi or HCMC); waits are 4–8 weeks, so book in early August if possible
- [ ] Prepare the visa file: passport, admission letter, Sperrkonto proof, IELTS, APS if required, health insurance, photos, motivation letter

- [ ] Handle work notice, apartment and personal affairs in Vietnam

## Oct 2027 — M.Sc. starts
