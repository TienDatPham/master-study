**Critical barriers for Dan:**
1. **On-campus aptitude test (Munich, Jul–Sep):** Dan is in Vietnam until WiSe 2027 start. Travel to Munich for the test alone costs €1,000–1,500 (flights + accommodation).
2. **GRE required:** Sit in Hanoi/HCMC, prep 2–3 months, fee ~USD 220.
3. **APS certificate:** Confirm with TUM if German B.Sc. exempts Vietnamese nationals; if not, processing takes 6–10 weeks + €80.

**Munich cost of living:**
- Rent: €900–1,200/month; Studentenwerk dorms €350–550/month (waitlist 1–2+ years)
- Total monthly: ~€1,200–1,600
- Best Werkstudent market in Germany (€15–25/hr IT roles)
- BMW, Siemens, SAP, Microsoft DACH, Google Munich

**Verdict: High reward, high logistical cost. Apply only if willing to fly to Munich in summer 2027 for aptitude test. Fourth-choice.**
