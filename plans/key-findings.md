## 3. Baden-Württemberg Non-EU Tuition

**Law:** Landeshochschulgebührengesetz Baden-Württemberg (LHGebG BW) — introduced 2017

**Fee:** €1,500/semester for non-EU international students at ALL BW public universities

**Affected programs in this research:**
- Universität Stuttgart M.Sc. Computer Science
- KIT M.Sc. Computer Science
- Universität Freiburg M.Sc. Computer Science
- Universität Heidelberg M.Sc. Data and Computer Science
- Universität Tübingen M.Sc. Machine Learning

**Exemptions (none apply to Dan for first M.Sc.):**
- EU/EEA citizenship ✗
- German Abitur (Hochschulzugangsberechtigung from German school) ✗ (Dan's HZQ is from Vietnam)
- Completed BOTH a German B.Sc. AND German M.Sc. ✗ (Dan only has the B.Sc.; the M.Sc. is what he's applying for)
- Settlement permit holders ✗
- Scholarship holders (some exceptions) — possible if Deutschlandstipendium or equivalent

**Total extra cost if attending a BW university:** 4 × €1,500 = **€6,000** in tuition alone

**Monitoring note:** Political pressure to repeal or reduce BW non-EU tuition has grown since 2023; check annually before application (WiSe 2027/28 applications in early 2027).

## 4. APS Certificate (Akademische Prüfstelle)

The APS is a German embassy document verification process for Vietnamese applicants that authenticates school and university transcripts.

| University | APS requirement |
| --- | --- |
| TUM | ✅ Explicitly required for Vietnamese nationals |
| Others | Likely waived when primary degree is a German B.Sc. — confirm individually |

**Why Dan may be exempt at non-TUM universities:** His primary qualification for M.Sc. admission is a German Zeugnis (TU Dresden B.Sc.) — an official German document. Universities typically only require APS for original Vietnamese qualifications.

**Process if required:** Apply via German Embassy Vietnam (Hanoi or HCMC); cost ~€80; processing 6–10 weeks; apply early.

## 9. Werkstudent (Working During Studies)

- **Maximum:** 20 hours/week during semester; full-time during semester breaks (Semesterferien)
- **Gross hourly rates (IT/RPA/automation):** €15–25/hour
- **Monthly gross (20 h/week × ~4.3 weeks):** ~€1,290–2,150
- **Tax:** With Lohnsteuerklasse 1, a Werkstudent earning <€538/month pays no social insurance contributions. Above that, student health insurance (Krankenkasse) applies.
- **Best cities for Werkstudent IT roles:** Munich > Berlin > Stuttgart/Karlsruhe > Dresden > Bonn
- **Dan's skills (RPA, Power BI, SQL, Power Platform):** In demand across all these cities. RPA specifically sought by consulting firms, insurance, banking, and logistics companies.
